Docs

Your work lives in a vault. What your agent learns lives in a private cortex beside it. Start with the workspace, learn how memory and skill approval work, then connect your agent. The architecture and API reference describe the code; Status and limits separates what is built from what is still planned.

Reading as an agent? Every page is also plain Markdown: start at llms.txt.

ohm is in development. These docs cover the implementation and the work still planned. Built does not mean verified on the live service; see Status and limits.

Get started

  • Quick start. Start with your posts, then open the private cortex your agent uses to remember your work.
  • Sign in. You sign in to ohm with an ohm account, made with Google, Microsoft or Bluesky.

Workspace

  • The workspace. Use the rail to switch between your vault of posts and your private cortex.
  • Posts. Everything you make in the vault is a post.
  • Labels. Labels are the sections of your work, in your own words.

Sharing

  • Who sees a post. Every post is marked private, for friends, or public.
  • Publish. Publish makes what other people see match your workspace.
  • Friends. Friends-only sharing is planned.

Folder

  • Sync with a folder. The command-line sync brings your posts and a local folder of Markdown files into line.
  • Posts as files. In your folder, each post is one Markdown file.
  • Export and leaving. You can export your vault posts as Markdown files.

Cortex

  • Your cortex. Your vault holds what you make.
  • Memory and notes. A memory is something worth carrying forward.
  • Skills and tools. A skill tells an agent how to do a task.

Agent

  • Your agent. Connect the coding agent you already use to your vault and cortex.
  • Finding people. Finding people is planned.

Architecture

  • System overview. ohm keeps a vault of posts and a private cortex for agent knowledge.
  • Identity and access. A member has two identities.
  • Data model. Posts and cortex knowledge are separate data.
  • Mirror and publish. Two exchanges connect a vault to its member's repository.
  • Agent integration. An agent reaches a vault through an MCP server that runs on the member's own machine.
  • Service API. The vault and cortex service registers 38 requests in 15 capabilities.

Reference

  • Where your things live. Public posts live in your AT Protocol repository.
  • Status and limits. What is built, what has been tested and what remains planned.
