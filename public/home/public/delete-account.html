Delete your account

You can delete your ohm account at any time. Your email stays in your Gmail or Microsoft mailbox.

In the app

Open Settings, choose Account, then Delete account. ohm asks you to sign in again to confirm, then deletes the account straight away.

Without the app

Write to support@collisions.co from an email address connected to your ohm account and ask us to delete it. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in methods and every signed-in device.
  • ohm's access to your mailboxes, which ohm revokes where the provider allows.
  • The message index, sync state, your conversations with the publicist, and everything ohm learned about you and the people you write to.

Nothing is kept, except copies in backups, which are gone within 30 days. The email in your mailbox is not touched.
