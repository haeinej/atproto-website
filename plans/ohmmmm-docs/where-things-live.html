Before you rely on it

Where your things live

ohm keeps a working copy of your posts, private ones included, so that you and your agent can work from anywhere. Your originals live with you.

Three places

What is thereWhose it is
Your account on the open networkYour public posts, and your identityYours. It can be hosted by ohm, by Bluesky, or on a server you run, and it can be moved
Your folderEvery post that is not public, as Markdown filesYours, on your machine or in your own cloud storage
Your workspace on ohmA working copy of all of itohm holds it for you

By who can see the post

The originalohm's working copyWho ohm shows it to
PublicYour account, in the openYesEveryone
FriendsYour folder, once connectedYesYou, and your friends
PrivateYour folder, once connectedYesOnly you

What ohm can read

Everything in your workspace, private posts included. An editor and an agent need the whole of your work to be useful, so ohm holds all of it.

  • ohm shows your private posts to nobody.
  • ohm does not use your private posts to match you with other people.
  • A private post is never written to your account on the open network. Everything there can be read by anyone.

The promise is not that ohm cannot read your posts. It is that nothing depends on ohm keeping them.

With and without a folder

With a folder connected, deleting ohm's copy loses nothing. Until you connect one, ohm holds the only copy of your private and friends-only posts. Sync with a folder
